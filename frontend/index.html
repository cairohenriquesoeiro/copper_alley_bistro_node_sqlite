Our Story

Born in a converted copper workshop on Copper Lane, we blend Irish heritage with contemporary technique.

Craft, Warmth, Community

Chef-owner Maeve Collins opened Copper Alley in 2019 with a simple idea: honest produce, cooked over fire, served in a space that feels like home. Exposed brick, hand-beaten copper bar, and low lighting set the stage for long evenings.

We work directly with Wicklow farms and Howth fishermen. Our menu changes with the tide and the season.
